# AI assistance

Aymen Ochi is the software author and maintainer; Abdullah Muzil Alharbi is a scientific coauthor with roles in AUTHORS.md.

ChatGPT and Codex (OpenAI) were used as assistive tools for troubleshooting technical problems, debugging and testing analysis code, checking consistency between numerical outputs and manuscript text, and supporting manuscript drafting and revision. ChatGPT was also used to simulate critical reviewer feedback during internal manuscript preparation, helping identify potential methodological ambiguities, reporting omissions and interpretive weaknesses. These exercises were internal checks and did not constitute independent peer review. No synthetic research observations were generated, and all statistical figures were produced programmatically from computed results. The human authors retain responsibility for the research, the verification of AI-assisted outputs, and the final methods, interpretations and conclusions.

Human review and final approval of this revised version remain required before submission.
